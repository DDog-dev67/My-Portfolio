What Dimensions I Used for Each Viewport and Why:
For phone I used a min-width of 461px and a max-width of 768px, for tablet I used a min-width of 768px and max-width of 1024px, 
and finally for desktop its 1024px and up. The reasons I used these dimensions is because these are the average dimensions for 
each type of device and therefore should work across most devices.

Where I used gradient:
I used gradient for all buttons (nav buttons and play/pause button for video) across all pages.

What Color Scheme I Used and Why:
I used a black background and lime-green text across all pages because I wanted to simulate a matrix/hacker feel to the site, as well as 
make it readable and look smooth.
