My Projects

This is a list of 4 of my projects that I've worked on as well as a brief description of each.

1. TrueNorthSec

A site I created with the purpose of making cybersecurity concepts/terms as simplistic as possible. I also add cybersecurity news reports from time to time.

2. NorthernOverwatch

A site that my father started and I helped with, that acts as a magazine with different cybersecurity stories explained as simply as possible for everyone to understand. There is also a subscription service that gives subscribers access to all of our content that regular users do not get.

3. Matrix Music Video

A project I worked on in my grade 12 communications technology class. The goal of the project was to demonstrate effective use of video editing software (Adobe Premiere, Davinci Resolve, etc) and create a music video that includes recorded clips with seamless transitions, appropriate music choice, as well as good cinematogrophy.

4. 1st year Networking I Final Project

This was our final project in my year 1 Intro to Networking I class. In this project, we had to basically create our own network with a specific number of devices, using various techniques and skills we picked up throughout the year, such as subnetting. This project was made using Cisco Packet Tracer, which is used to design, build, and troubleshoot virtual computer networks without the use of physical hardware.
